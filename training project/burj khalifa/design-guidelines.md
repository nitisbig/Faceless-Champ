# Burj Khalifa — Visual Design Guidelines

## 1. Visual Style
- **Theme:** Cinematic architectural engineering.
- **Techniques:** 3D wireframe, CAD blueprints, structural cutaways, exploded views, and engineering simulations.
- **Background:** Dark navy `#0B1020`.
- **Colors:** Cyan for geometry, amber for highlights, orange for forces, white for labels.
- Clean, minimal, technically accurate visuals.

## 2. Composition
- Format: 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 FPS.
- Building/model occupies 65–75% of the frame.
- Maintain 5–8% safe margins.
- One primary engineering concept per scene.
- Avoid clutter, decorative UI, and unnecessary elements.

## 3. Typography
- Fonts: Inter, IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Mono.
- Titles: 42–52 px.
- Labels: 24–30 px.
- Maximum 3–5 annotations per scene.
- Short labels, clear dimension lines, consistent alignment.

## 4. Architecture Visualization
- **Wireframe:** Reveal exterior and internal geometry.
- **Cutaway/X-ray:** Expose core, structural walls, floors, and foundations.
- **Blueprint:** Show accurate floor plans and dimensions.
- **Exploded View:** Separate structural components.
- **Simulation:** Visualize wind, load distribution, and structural behavior.
- **Solid CAD:** Show concrete, steel, and glass materials.

## 5. Camera & Animation
- Smooth orbit, zoom, vertical tracking, and sectional reveals.
- Use slow, cinematic movements with natural easing.
- Keep the camera steady during complex explanations.
- Highlight components sequentially.
- Synchronize every animation with narration.

## 6. Scene Sequence
1. **Introduction:** Full building wireframe, 828 m height.
2. **Geometry:** Y-shaped floor plan and three wings.
3. **Structure:** Buttressed core and load distribution.
4. **Aerodynamics:** Wind flow and spiraling setbacks.
5. **Foundation:** Concrete raft and deep piles.
6. **Construction:** Structural assembly and façade.
7. **Final Reveal:** Wireframe transitions into complete building.

## 7. Engineering Accuracy
- Maintain recognizable building proportions.
- Show physically plausible force directions and structural connections.
- Use simplified technical models when exact specifications are unavailable.
- Avoid invented numerical simulation results.
- Verify dimensions and structural details.

## 8. Quality Standards
- Sharp, antialiased geometry.
- Consistent lighting, colors, and typography.
- No overlapping labels or excessive wireframe density.
- Smooth transitions and clear visual hierarchy.
- Every animation must explain an engineering concept.

**Core Principle:** Show the entire structure → isolate a component → explain its engineering function → reconnect it to the complete building.
